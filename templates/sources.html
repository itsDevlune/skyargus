What the images mean

SPHEREx IMAGE arrays contain calibrated surface brightness. Argus preserves native FITS files, SHA-256 checksums and original archive URLs. Display cutouts are reprojected to a common north-up, east-left TAN grid with 6.2″ pixels. Uncovered pixels stay transparent. Categorical raw flags use nearest-neighbor interpolation. Header-provided flag names and bitmasks are decoded as informational, caution or invalid for photometry. Display previews still exclude only NaN/uncovered pixels; a finalized scientific masking policy and quality overlay remain unfinished.

A shared asinh stretch produces all sequence previews. Display contrast never changes FITS measurements. Display-grid files do not include propagated variance and must not be used as validated photometric products. Reprojection alone does not establish measured alignment. Local filter response varies across the detector: same detector does not establish matched wavelengths. The viewer labels comparisons “review needed”. No orbit, hazard, distance or discovery probability is inferred from marks.

Current limitations: quantitative spectra, calibrated differences, detector composites, WISE reference imagery, guided real-data activities and calibrated NEOSSat imaging remain unfinished. Manual apparent-motion fits use explicitly assumed positional errors. See docs/FEATURES.md and the release checklist for evidence and exact status.

Access, privacy and retention

Public scientific cutouts are shared cache artifacts. Candidate notes, investigations and report settings are private to a secure server-side browser session. Clearing cookies loses editing access. Reports are immutable snapshots; sharing creates a separate revocable read capability. All read links for a report can be revoked from Investigations. Revocation cannot retract downloaded copies. Saved reports pin their sequence. Automatic cache eviction is not enabled.
