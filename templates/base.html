A closer look at a changing sky

1. Resolve an object or enter RA/Dec, then choose a field width and detector.

2. Load observations. Compare dated frames on a fixed sky grid. A changing filter response can mimic brightness changes.

3. Choose Mark, select a source, write a note, then save an immutable evidence report.

FOV changes archive selection; zoom changes only the display. Arrow keys step frames. Space plays or pauses. Shortcuts pause while typing.

Your private annotations belong to this browser session. Clearing cookies loses editing access. Export reports to keep a copy.

Argus does not confirm discoveries, hazards, or orbits.
